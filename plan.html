What To Bring / Planning Tips

A little planning can make your first theme park visit easier and more enjoyable. Check the park's hours, weather, and policies before you go, and pack a few essentials to help you stay comfortable throughout the day.

Our Suggestions:

  • Comfortable shoes
  • Sunscreen
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Phone and portable charger
  • Secure bag or zippered pockets for loose items

Safety & Accessibility

Every roller coaster has its own safety requirements and accessibility guidelines. Before riding, check the posted information and ask a park employee if you have any questions or concerns.

  • Follow all posted height and safety requirements.
  • Listen to ride operators and follow their instructions.
  • Keep loose items secured while riding.
  • Check the park's accessibility guide for information about specific rides.
  • Ask a park employee about accommodations or boarding options if needed.
