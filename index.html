Welcome to First Drop

First Drop is a beginner’s guide to roller coasters and theme parks. Whether you’re getting ready for your first coaster or planning your first theme park visit, you’ll find information to help you know what to expect and feel more prepared before you go.

Roller coasters can be intimidating when you don’t know what to expect. First Drop breaks down different coaster types, planning tips, and safety information so you can learn the basics before heading to the park.

You don’t need to be a coaster expert to enjoy a theme park. Start with what interests you, learn at your own pace, and use First Drop to help make your first visit feel a little less overwhelming.
